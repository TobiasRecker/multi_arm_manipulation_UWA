What was smoothed and checked

The design comparison uses joint caps of 0.45 rad/s, 0.4 rad/s² and 2 rad/s³. These are experiment settings, not measured hardware limits. The resulting analytical jerk bound is lower than that cap.

Velocity ramps use a quintic function and join a constant-speed plateau. Position through jerk is continuous, with zero velocity, acceleration and jerk at each primitive endpoint. The stop before the vertical approach remains: removing that geometric corner would require blending the path.

Straight joint paths stay exact. Cartesian joint interpolation changes from a cubic to a quintic spline through the same IK knots; targets and orientation stay fixed within checked tolerances. Geometry differences, static/self/carton checks and a new combined timeline are evaluated explicitly.

Some individual phases accelerate more strongly than the baseline's very slow joint transit, while the previously sharp short approaches accelerate much less. The global acceleration and jerk peaks fall and total duration decreases. This is redistribution of the available envelope, not a uniform slowdown.

Open grippers, fixed bases, no contact dynamics, no real robot motion. Stopping assumptions remain unqualified diagnostics. This is an offline kinematic comparison, not a hardware execution approval.
