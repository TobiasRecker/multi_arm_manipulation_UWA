How to interpret the 10× test

All four trajectories are sped up by the same factor. Therefore their joint configuration combination at t/10 equals the original at t; the geometric distance history is unchanged. Individual speed factors or different start offsets would require another joint collision check.

The 10× velocity bounds remain below the velocity values in the saved URDF. They exceed the original deliberately slow 0.05 rad/s profile. That profile is not the physical maximum of a UR. Acceleration, jerk, torque, tool payload and servo tracking still need an appropriate execution envelope; none is qualified by this geometric preview.

Distance thresholds now use commanded joint velocities at each instant, with the original speed floor and unchanged latency/braking assumptions. The original braking acceleration (0.5 rad/s²) and delay assumptions are not measured guarantees. The conservative model can demand large separations; exceeding it is not proof that the robot physically cannot perform the motion.

Open grippers, fixed bases and no payload lift. These are planned polynomial curves, not native UR MoveJ/MoveL commands or a physics simulation. Reported derivatives are analytical upper bounds for the curves, not measured motor data.
