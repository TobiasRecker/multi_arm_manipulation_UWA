What this test establishes — and what comes next

Independent paths were sampled against each arm's self geometry, the tables, carton and all fixed base bodies. Other moving arms are considered in the separate simultaneous-start diagnostic. Timing coordination is step 4 and remains unresolved.

All candidate solutions use the same target position and orientation per arm: nominal grasp centre 15 mm below the upper corner, gripper axis vertically downward, jaws oriented diagonally across the corner. The nominal TCP lies 145 mm along the gripper base +Z; it is not a calibrated physical grasp TCP. Grippers stay open. Closing, holding force and lifting have not been tested.

The primitives are quintic joint motions and Cartesian-line IK splines with zero velocity and acceleration at joins. These are MoveJ/MoveL-style geometric planning primitives, not native UR commands. Dense samples are not continuous swept-volume certification. Model bases, carton shape and object pose retain their documented approximation limits.

The rejected all-zero start intersects the right table with the left-front wrist/gripper. It is shown only as a diagnostic and is never connected to the planned paths.
