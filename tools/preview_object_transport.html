Model assumptions and validation scope

This is an offline kinematic experiment with hypothetical rigid grasps. The previous open-finger geometry is retained; no physical grip or load-bearing contact is simulated. Nominal carton clearance above the table is 3 mm at the start and end. Lowering is not proof of supported placement.

All 24 joints share a C3 time law with full stops between lifting, horizontal travel and lowering. Joint interpolation is checked against all four fixed object-to-TCP transformations. The moving carton, environment and robot self collision are checked with FCL; six arm-pair margins use the sphere envelopes. Results are discrete samples, not a continuous collision certificate.

The Jacobian indicator uses a 0.5 m characteristic length. Its trial threshold is 0.05; this is not a universal singularity or load-capacity threshold. Forces, torques, grasp stiffness, internal loading and hardware controller tracking are outside this experiment.

The local optimizer uses analytical distance gradients and six yaw spline controls bounded by ±15°. Object translation is fixed. Environmental collisions and singularity indicators are acceptance checks, not gradient constraints. A translational detour or different IK branch is not implemented here. Conservative stopping reserves remain a separate diagnostic; these offline results do not enable hardware execution.
